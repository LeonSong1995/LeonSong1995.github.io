About

      
    
        
  • 
          Hi, I am Liyang. I received my Ph.D. in statistical genetics from
          Westlake University
          (joint program with Zhejiang University) in 2026, advised by Prof.
          Jian Yang.
          I am now a postdoctoral researcher in the Yang Lab at Westlake University.
        
    • 
        
  • 
          My research sits at the intersection of statistical genetics and
          artificial intelligence. I develop statistical models and deep-learning
          methods that integrate single-cell and spatial omics data with human genetics, to identify
          the cell states and tissue locations associated with complex traits and diseases. Topics include:
          
      
            
    • Integrating GWAS with spatial and single-cell transcriptomics
      • 
            
    • Graph neural networks and deep learning for omics data
      • 
            
    • Mixed models and cellular deconvolution
      • 
            
    • Scalable, open-source software for large-scale genomic data
      • 
          
    
        
    • 
        
  • 
          I developed gsMap
          (Nature, 2025), which maps trait-associated cells in tissue by combining GWAS summary
          statistics with spatial transcriptomics, and
          MeDuSA
          (Nature Computational Science, 2023), a mixed-model method for estimating cell-state
          abundance from bulk RNA-seq.
        
    • 
        
  • 
          I am always open to discussions and collaborations — feel free to reach out via
          email. :)

Software

      

        

          

            gsMap
            Python
          

          
Genetically informed spatial mapping of cells for complex traits. Integrates GWAS summary statistics with spatial transcriptomics to map trait-associated cells in tissue.

          

            ★ 229
            docs
            pypi
          

        

        

          

            MeDuSA
            R
          

          
Fine-resolution cellular deconvolution. Uses an scRNA-seq reference and a linear mixed model to estimate cell-state abundance along a trajectory from bulk RNA-seq.

          

            ★ 46
            github
          

        

        

          

            gsMap3D
            Python
          

          
Extends gsMap to 3D spatial transcriptomics atlases: mapping the cellular etiology of complex traits in 3D tissue architecture. Manuscript under revision.
